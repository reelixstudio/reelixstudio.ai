01Location master+

Original production prompt. Replace numbered references with your own approved assets.

PURPOSE: One single photorealistic LOCATION MASTER image for Danney's ancient Egyptian morning comedy. Landscape16:9, one uninterrupted WIDE SHOT from a THREE-QUARTER CORNER camera position. No collage, grid, split screen, panels or alternate views.
REFERENCE MAP: Reference1 is actual DNA5.5s: weathered Egyptian walls and contemporary service window integrated naturally. Reference2 is DNA7.5s: ancient kitchen materials and practical warm sunlight/shadow. Reference3 is DNA9.0s: sandy market architecture, fabric awnings and everyday modern commerce. Copy their photographic texture, materials, light and historical-modern coexistence. Ignore source people, horses, chariot, food action and exact original composition.
LOCATION: A royal Egyptian sandstone courtyard opening directly onto an ancient-modern market street, with its cafe/kitchen in the rear wall. About60percent ancient and40percent modern in FEEL: Egyptian columns, two weathered royal statues, carved aged stone and plaster, timber lintels, linen awnings, clay vessels and a monumental sundial mixed with visibly functional modern retail infrastructure. An OPEN stainless cafe service hatch with counter, dark glass shop opening, plain blank menu/display panel, exposed utility conduit and a modern faucet in the stone washbasin. Make these modern features readable rather than tiny decoration. No futuristic city or pristine shopping mall. Warm sandy world, ordinary modern conveniences.
MASTER CAMERA: Camera at human eye height in the SOUTH-EAST street/entrance corner, looking diagonally NORTH-WEST into the courtyard. About24mm photographic perspective with natural architecture and no fisheye. Show foreground street and a clear diagonal longboard route through south gateway, both adjacent wall planes and enough courtyard floor to establish scale. Same image includes royal columns/statues framing entry, north cafe doorway and service counter at rear, east stone washbasin on nearer/right side, west monumental sundial farther/left. Kitchen implied through open doorway/hatch; do not invent a separate interior viewpoint in this image.
GROUND AND GEOGRAPHY: Courtyard about14mwide by10mdeep; market street EAST-WEST immediately SOUTH. NORTH wall: doorway WEST of service hatch, connected cafe/kitchen behind. EAST basin, WEST sundial and stone ledge. Statues flank SOUTH gate. Entry street and courtyard floor are continuous and LEVEL: no entry steps, stair, raised sill or obstructing curb across the longboard path. Weathered paving/dust retains authentic texture. Preserve usable open rolling space; no hero props yet.
LIGHT AND LOOK: Actual DNA's warm dusty sunlight, sandy ochre/cream/earth colours, slightly muted contrast, candid grainy photographed film feel, imperfect worn surfaces. Consistent morning sun fromSOUTHEAST, naturally shaded recesses. Avoid glossyHDR, oversharpenedCGI or sterile resort lighting.
TEXT: No words, labels, brand names or watermark; any modern menu/display panel stays blank.
ACCEPTANCE: ONE SINGLE wide3/4corner view; royalcourtyard+marketstreet clearlyconnected; readable ancient-modernmix; level longboardroute; nocharacters/animals/vehicles/longboard/toothbrush/espresso machine/watch. Once THIS master is approved, other camera perspectives may be generated from it only for locations reused in2+approvedshots.

PURPOSE: One photorealistic 16:9 film character reference sheet of Danney dressed as the royal Egyptian character in reference image 2. Exactly three equal panels in a single horizontal row with thin dividers.
REFERENCE MAP:

IMAGE 2 is an ACTUAL selected DNA frame at 6.0 seconds. Copy its WARDROBE only: striped Egyptian headcloth, broad gold collar, white draped linen/pleated wrap garment and restrained gold accents. Ignore its actor's identity, chariot, reins, cup, horses, pose and background. Do not recreate this frame's scene or import a second person.
SUBJECT AND WARDROBE: Same Danney in all three panels, natural lean adult build, calm deadpan closed-mouth expression, naturally open eyes, realistic skin pores and tonal variation. Dark-and-light striped nemes-style cloth over crown and draping down either side of neck/upper shoulders, worn gold trim; entire face unobscured. Broad gold collar as in DNA, white draped linen at shoulders and a pleated white linen wrap kilt to around knee level, understated matching gold accents on upper arms/waist. Plain brown leather strap sandals. Tactile woven cloth, natural folds and lightly worn metal like a real photographed costume. Keep wrists accessible for later watch and prop handling. No extra fantasy armour, mask or giant crown. Hands empty. Exactly same face/headgear/collar/linen/gold accents/sandals across panels.
COMPOSITION:

CENTRE: LEFT-FACING MEDIUM. Whole head down to upper thighs. Standing upright. Head, chest, shoulders and hips rotate about 40 degrees toward the IMAGE'S OUTER LEFT EDGE. Nose points LEFT; right cheek visible; right shoulder nearer camera. Gaze left, full headcloth visible.

04Longboard performance+

Original production prompt. Replace numbered references with your own approved assets.

A photorealistic seven-second continuous longboard performance in the approved ancient Egyptian construction courtyard. Begin exactly from the supplied start image: Danney in the approved striped headcloth and white linen, both sandals on the same neutral black-grip wooden longboard with orange wheels, crane and hanging obelisk behind/right. Original face reference defines his exact clean-shaven adult identity; preserve the accepted start-frame outfit, proportions and quiet deadpan expression. Board image preserves rigid deck/trucks/wheels. Original DNA image supplies muted dusty sunlight and fine photographic grain only. Video reference1 supplies cross-step FOOTWORK ONLY; video reference2 supplies nose-loaded pivot mechanics ONLY. Keep our actor/location/outfit, natural real-time speed and camera; do not copy reference street, people or tutorial edits. FIRST0–1s: Danney rolls naturally along the clear image-left lane with one shallow smooth carve, knees soft, hips balanced over board. 1–3s: one fluent cross-step—front foot adjusts, rear foot crosses over it, then sequentially uncrosses back into balanced stance; one planted supporting foot during each moving-foot transition. 3–5s: one controlled180-degree nose pivot—front foot/weight over front truck/nose, shoulders guide turn, rear truck lifts briefly and tail arcs around the grounded front contact, then all wheels return to ground. 5–7s: steady composed roll-out with balanced feet, maintaining momentum and deadpan face. A ground-level three-quarter side-follow camera matches riding speed and keeps entire head, both feet, full deck and wheel contacts readable throughout. One uninterrupted shot with finite actions at normal speed. Board behaves as one rigid physical object; wheels spin/turn and contact ground, feet attach naturally, linen/headcloth react subtly to motion. Background workers continue rope guidance/stonework, load remains behind and separate from the riding lane. Preserve source soft texture rather than glossy render. No voice, music or sound: silent output. Avoid foot skating through deck, extra limbs, rubber feet, teleporting, hovering or transforming the board; action clarity takes priority over extra camera tricks.
