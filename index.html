Questions

Is the video AI-generated?
Yes, it is AI-assisted. We use your listing photos as the reference and generate motion, people and atmosphere around them, then edit the result by hand. Every shot is reviewed before it reaches you.
What do I need to provide?
Listing photos you have the rights to use, plus anything you want highlighted. You confirm you are authorized to share the materials.
What counts as a revision?
Changes within the approved concept, such as shot order, timing, music, text or swapping a shot. A different creative direction is a new concept.
Can I get a refund?
Full refund before you approve the concept, 50% after concept approval, and no refund once the final video is delivered. Full details are in our terms.
