01

Imagine: your saves, sorted.

Everything you save from Pinterest, YouTube and Instagram, plus your own files, goes in as a mess. Click the ring and it sorts by what each picture is doing. Hover a source to see which pictures came from it.

02

Break it down: the video comes apart.

Keep scrolling. A finished video splits into its parts: footage, style and light, motion, sound, story. We swap some, add one of our own, and it goes back together as our film.

Reference: KPop Demon Hunters (Sony Pictures Animation / Netflix), shown for analysis · Our film: Last Train, Reelix Studio · Watch the reference clip ↗

03

Swap the rules: keep what works.

The style comes down to five rules. Each row is their shot on the left and ours on the right. Hover a row to see ours; click to keep it in.

Reference frames: KPop Demon Hunters (Sony Pictures Animation / Netflix), shown for analysis · Our film: Last Train, Reelix Studio · Watch the reference clip ↗

04

Concepts: three ways to tell it.

The rules become three different looks for the same story. You pick one before anything gets built.

Hover a board to look around it. Click one to pick it.

05

Storyboard: 18 pictures, no video yet.

Every shot is one still. You approve all of them first, so nothing gets animated that you haven't seen.

Shot 1 of 18

06

Motion: the still starts to move.

Each approved frame goes to the video model with its sheets. Drag the bar to see the frame on one side and the moving shot on the other.
